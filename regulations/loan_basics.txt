Title: Loan Basics

What is a Loan:
A loan is money borrowed from a bank that must be repaid with interest over time.

Types of Loans:
- Personal loan
- Home loan
- Car loan
- Education loan

Eligibility:
- Stable income
- Good credit score
- Employment or business proof

Steps to Apply:
- Choose loan type
- Check eligibility
- Submit application and documents
- Bank verifies credit score
- Loan approved and amount disbursed

Important Points:
- EMI must be paid on time
- Interest rates vary by bank
- Defaulting affects credit score
