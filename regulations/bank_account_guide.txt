Title: Bank Account Opening Guide

What is a Bank Account:
A bank account allows you to deposit, withdraw, and manage money securely.

Types of Accounts:
- Savings Account: For individuals
- Current Account: For businesses
- Fixed Deposit: For savings with interest

Documents Required:
- Aadhaar card or identity proof
- PAN card
- Address proof
- Passport-size photographs

Steps to Open:
- Choose bank and account type
- Visit branch or apply online
- Fill account opening form
- Submit documents
- Complete KYC verification
- Deposit minimum balance (if required)
- Receive account details and debit card

Important Points:
- Maintain minimum balance if required
- Update KYC regularly
- Keep account details secure
