Title: Credit Card Guide

What is a Credit Card:
A credit card allows you to borrow money from a bank to make purchases and repay later. You get a credit limit based on your income and credit profile.

Eligibility:
- Minimum age: 18–21 years (varies by bank)
- Stable income (salary or business)
- Good credit score (typically 700–750+)
- Existing relationship with bank can help

Documents Required:
- PAN card (mandatory)
- Aadhaar card or address proof
- Income proof (salary slips or ITR)
- Passport-size photographs

Steps to Apply:
- Choose a card based on rewards, cashback, or travel benefits
- Apply online via bank website or visit branch
- Fill application form with personal and financial details
- Submit required documents
- Bank verifies your credit score and eligibility
- Card is approved and delivered to your address

Important Points:
- Always pay dues on time to avoid interest
- Interest rates are high if unpaid
- Late payments affect your credit score
- Use within your limit to maintain good financial health
