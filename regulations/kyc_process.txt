Title: KYC Process

What is KYC:
KYC (Know Your Customer) is a mandatory process used by banks to verify identity and prevent fraud.

Documents Required:
- Identity proof (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport)
- Address proof (utility bill, rental agreement)

Types of KYC:
- Full KYC: Complete verification at branch
- e-KYC: Online verification using Aadhaar OTP
- Video KYC: Verification through video call

Steps:
- Submit documents
- Bank verifies identity
- Biometric or OTP verification may be required
- Account becomes fully active after verification

Importance:
- Prevents fraud and money laundering
- Mandatory for banking services
